    • HARDIK HIMANSHU PANDYA

    Hardik Pandya is an Indian international all-rounder, captain of Mumbai Indians in IPL 2026, and a key player for India in limited-overs cricket. Personal Background Hardik Himanshu Pandya was born on 11 October 1993 in Choryasi, Surat, Gujarat, India. He grew up in a middle-class family; his father, Himanshu Pandya, ran a small car finance business and later moved the family to Vadodara to provide better cricket training opportunities for Hardik and his elder brother, Krunal Pandya. Hardik trained at the Kiran More Cricket Academy and left formal education after ninth grade to focus on cricket. He is known for his flamboyant personality, tattoos, and fashion sense, and was previously engaged to actress Natasa Stankovic, with whom he has a son, Agastya. Wikipedia +2 Domestic and IPL Career Pandya debuted in domestic cricket for Baroda in 2013 and played a pivotal role in winning the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy in 2013–14. He made his IPL debut in 2015 with Mumbai Indians, quickly establishing himself as a match-winner with explosive batting and effective medium-fast bowling. He contributed to Mumbai Indians’ IPL titles in 2015, 2017, 2019, and 2020. In 2022, he captained the Gujarat Titans to their maiden IPL title and led them to the final again in 2023. He returned to Mumbai Indians in 2024 as captain, though the team struggled that season.
